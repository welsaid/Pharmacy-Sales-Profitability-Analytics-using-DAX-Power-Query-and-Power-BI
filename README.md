# Pharmacy Sales Profitability Analytics Report

### Project Overview
A major pharmacy chain distributor operating across multiple European countries seeks to improve the overall performance of pharmacies in operation by implementing the right strategies and policies. This project analyses the business data to uncover patterns in transactions, comparing profitability against volume and recommending data−driven solutions that encourages both growth and scalability.

---
### Problem Statement
Some pharmacies have consistently underperformed over the past two years, while others have outperformed over the same period. This performance gap suggests underlying structural or operational drivers. The next step is to isolate the key factors that explain the variance and assess whether underperforming pharmacies can replicate the high-performing pharmacies’ practices in a scalable way.

---
### Business Questions Answered
- Are there differences in seasonal pattern against the overall trend?
-	Which countries and regions contribute the most to revenue and profitability?
-	Are poor performances due to the market or poor execution? 
-	How do promoted sales compare to non-promoted sales in performance
-	Are there visible geographical differences in profitability?

---
### Tools and Methodology
- Tools Used
  - Excel at the initial stage in understanding the data structure.
  - Power Query for data cleansing to ensure accuracy and data integrity.
  - DAX (Data Analysis Expression) for calculated metric and custom logic.

- Methodology
  - Data Cleaning and Imputation:
    - Checked for skewedness in numerical distributions to help guide imputation method.
    - Standardized inconsistent text format to ensure the accuracy and reliability of the analysis.
    - Replaced values with more descriptive words to ensure insights are better communicated.
   
  - Data Modelling: Structured relationship between tables to enable smarter filtering, dynamic visualization and faster queries.

  - Assortment, Geographic & Comparative Analysis: Explored default performances across brands, categories, promoflags and geography.
 
  - Visual Storytelling: Used column charts, scatter plots, doughnut charts, maps and tables to communicate insights clearly.

### Key Insights
- Germany which is the most profitable country (€439M), accounted for the majority of revenue and demand (€1.5M and €75,677 respectively).
- While prescription drugs dominated transaction volume, non-promoted sales significantly outperformed promoted items, accounting for 92.5% of total profit. In contrast, promoted products contributed only 7.5%, suggesting limited profitability impact from promotional activities.
